Since xAI now owns X/Twitter, we are starting to see lots of AI features on X. But I argue that these features are not needed, because we already have a lot of magic that makes it fascinating.

## 1. Feed disappears, like magic

You go to X.com and nothing. Absolutely nothing. This happens so frequently lately. The only solution is to clean the cache from Chrome, specifically for X.com. I once mistakenly cleaned all stored cookies etc.

---

## 2. The statistics are magical

Below the post, it shows 115 retweets and 540 likes. If you click on the bar icon next to 43k, the pop-up shows that it was retweeted 113 times and liked 528 times, similar to what I saw on my phone.

![]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/uploads/2025/04/image-7.jpg)

![]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/uploads/2025/04/image-4.jpg)

Upon further investigation, this is because this post was edited once. The first version has 2 repots and 12 likes. These are precisely the differences between the two statistics. But some consistency would be nice.

---

## 3. The post disappears if you promote it!

For fun, I spent $20 to boost my post for two days. Immediately after I did that, I could not find the promoted post on my laptop. No matter how I tried to find the post, I could only see the replies. Pure magic!

---

## 4. Magic math ( N vs. N-1)

I set a promotion budget of $20 over 2 days. Initially, I noticed that it's going twice as fast as expected. It ended after 1 day. This screenshot explains it well. No logic can explain that $18/$20 spent in half of the time. Again, X engineers are magicians!

Of course, it's absolutely inconceivable that someone forgot to offset by 1 when calculating start and end times.

Based on these observations, I concluded that X is a magical place already. No AI needed.

Firing 80% of the engineers while still maintaining this level of magic is truly ... magical!
