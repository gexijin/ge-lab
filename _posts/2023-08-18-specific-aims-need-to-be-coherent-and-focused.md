A well-crafted grant proposal does more than outline a project; it states its objectives with clarity and precision. The Specific Aims section is pivotal because it defines what the project sets out to achieve.

The Specific Aims must be coherent and sharply focused. A grant proposal needs to convince reviewers to invest in your research. Like investors, funders are risk-averse: they favor well-defined, tightly focused projects over ambitious ones clouded with uncertainty.

When writing specific aims, keep the focus on presenting coherent, clear aims ([Santen et al., 2017](https://doi.org/10.1210/js.2017-00318)). Real examples can be illuminating, so let's look at three iterations of the same grant proposal.

## First Version: June 2018 (NIH Submission)

I proposed to:

1. Develop two bioinformatics tools (iDEP and ShinyGO) for analyzing genomics data
2. Expand the knowledge base supporting both tools
3. Use these tools to study gene regulation in embryonic development

Though noble, these aims were neither coherent nor focused; an entire career could be devoted to them. Unsurprisingly, the proposal was Not Discussed.

## Revised Version: November 2018 (NIH New Application)

I narrowed the aims to focus on iDEP, proposing to:

1. Improve reproducibility and add essential functionalities
2. Expand the knowledge base
3. Conduct testing and documentation
4. Create a standalone app to map raw RNA-Seq reads

This version earned an impact score of 57 (53rd percentile). It was progress, but it remained unfunded.

## Third Version: July 2019 (NIH Resubmission)

After reviewers criticized Specific Aim 4, I refined the goals further:

1. Redevelop and improve iDEP
2. Expand the knowledge base
3. Conduct testing, documentation, and user outreach

These aims were coherent, naturally related, and specifically tailored. Additional letters of support from users reinforced the proposal. With an impact score of 30 (15th percentile), this version was funded in September 2020.

## Conclusion: Clarity and Focus Are Key

These examples show how important it is to define and focus the aims of a grant proposal. Funders are attracted to projects with a clear, low-risk path to success. Emphasize coherence, and don't hesitate to narrow your aims to make a strong, persuasive case. As my own experience shows, diligence and precision in crafting your proposal pay off.
